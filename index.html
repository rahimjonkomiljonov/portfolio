01 / About

Engineer in the making

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Gachon University in South Korea with a strong interest in artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and software development. I enjoy solving problems and turning ideas into practical solutions.

It started with Python: a password manager, a Pomodoro timer and a habit tracker taught me how to break a problem into parts and finish it. Now I build larger systems, like a webcam tracker that measures audience attention, a real-time polling platform and a stock widget for Windows and Android.

I'm particularly interested in how AI can improve cybersecurity, protect sensitive information and address emerging digital threats.
